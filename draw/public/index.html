Rules & Formats

We are running two competitions at the same time, off the same scorecard, playing for bragging rights.

Team competition

Four-Ball Alliance (Stableford)

Who plays
Everyone.
Handicaps
Official Course Handicaps apply. Players without an official handicap play off a maximum 24 handicap.
Scoring
The two best Stableford scores count on every hole.

Individual competition

Individual Stableford

Who plays
Players with an official handicap only.
Scoring
Highest total individual Stableford points over 18 holes, using your full Course Handicap.

Pace of play

Pick up at net double bogey

Because both competitions use Stableford, everyone can pick up their ball once they can no longer score a point on a hole (net double bogey). This keeps the round moving smoothly.

Tie-breakers

If there is a tie in either the Team Alliance or the Individual Stableford, the winner is decided by comparing Stableford points in this order (standard R&A / GolfRSA count-out):

  1. Back 9Most points on holes 10–18
  2. Last 6Most points on holes 13–18
  3. Last 3Most points on holes 16–18
  4. 18th holeMost points on the 18th
